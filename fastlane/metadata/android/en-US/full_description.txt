Melogold is a free and open-source music player for YouTube Music. Use it without an account, or sign in to keep your favorites, library and playlists in sync across all your devices — on the public Melogold server or on your own self-hosted one.

<b>Features:</b>

* Play (almost) any song or video from YouTube Music
* Background playback and offline cache
* Search songs, albums, artists, videos and playlists
* Discover new music by mood and genre
* Import playlists from YouTube
* Plain and synchronized lyrics, with editing
* Material You colors (Android 12+) and artwork colors in the player
* Loudness normalization and SponsorBlock
* Android Auto
* Open YouTube and YouTube Music links (watch, playlist, channel and more)

<b>Coming soon:</b>

* Accounts with sync of favorites, library, playlists and history
* Sign in on a new device by scanning a QR code
