- The log is written to files and kept even if the system restarts the app. “Share” sends it as one file, not as text.
- Other → Troubleshooting: the log can be turned off, and you can choose how long to keep it — a day, 3 days, a week or a month. When it is off, nothing is written and what was written is deleted at once.
- On Android 17 (One UI 9) the sound may still go silent with the screen locked. The player now writes to the log what the system does to it: if the sound goes silent, send the log right after.
