- When a track is closed in your country, Melogold says so, and which country YouTube places you in. For example: "Unavailable in Russia: YouTube places you there, and the rights holder opened this track in 122 other countries". It used to say only "couldn't get the stream".
- The same behind a VPN: YouTube counts some VPN servers as Russia too, and now you see it at once. Pick a server in another country, or tap "Other versions".
- Storage explains what happens when a cache is full: new tracks and artwork take the place of old ones, there is nothing to do. Downloads are not part of the cache.
