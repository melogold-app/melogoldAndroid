- Voice control through Gemini (Android 16 and later): "Play Kino in Melogold", "Play my Favorites in Melogold". Gemini finds a song, artist, album or one of your playlists and plays it in Melogold, and it can also pause, resume and skip. Google is opening this to apps gradually, so it may not work on your phone right away.
- Google Assistant and Android Auto play music by voice with the same rules: your Library first, and only your Library without a network.
- A track first played from a link gets its cover, title and artist the next time it comes up.
- Library search treats «е» and «ё» the same: «еще» finds «Ещё раз».
