- After importing from ViTune, "Open history" takes you to your tracks. There is no ViTune "Songs" tab here: History plays its role, with "Recent" and "Most played".
- The History tile in the Library shows how many tracks you have played.
- The import summary counts only tracks the library shows: played, in Favorites or in playlists. ViTune also keeps the tracks of every album you opened; the summary no longer counts those.
- Settings › Database › "Library copy": "Save a copy" and "Import a copy". Melogold on Android opens the copy, and soon Windows and Apple devices will too. Your own lyrics and playlists survive the move.
