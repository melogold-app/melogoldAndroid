- Import from ViTune and ViMusic: Library › "Import from ViTune or ViMusic" or Settings › Database. The backup is added to your library: tracks, the whole listening history, Favorites, playlists, lyrics, saved albums and artists. Nothing is deleted, and importing again does not double the history. When you are signed in, the history reaches your other devices through the server.
- Music is always cached, up to 4 GB by default. When space runs out, the tracks not played for the longest time go first.
- A track fully in the cache plays offline and is marked as available offline. Downloads has an "In the cache" group.
- The cache lives in Android/data and is not cleaned up as junk. The old cache is cleared once on update.
- In lyrics mode, a tap at the bottom brings the controls back instead of seeking.
- The mini player's long-press menu has "Add to Favorites".
- The launch screen uses the system colors instead of dark blue.
