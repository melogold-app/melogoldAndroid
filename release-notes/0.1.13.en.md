- Insights: Library › "Insights" — how much you listened this week, month, year and all time, your top tracks, artists and albums, when you listened and what you discovered. In December, "Year in review" cards you can share.
- Links: "Share" on a track, album or playlist. Your own playlist becomes a link on your Melogold server: a friend with Melogold opens it in the app, without Melogold they get a page with the list and "Listen on YouTube". Your links are in Account.
- Spotify, Apple Music, Yandex Music, Deezer and Tidal links open in Melogold: paste one into Search.
- Remote: the "Device" button in the player controls the music and volume on another of your devices through the server; "Listen here" takes playback over. Turn it off in Settings › Player › "Control from other devices".
