- Your own title, artist and album of a track: track menu → "Edit details…". An album collected from different videos looks like one album on all your devices; "As on YouTube" puts everything back. While selecting, "Set album…" does it for several tracks at once.
- Actions on selected tracks: long-press a track in Favorites, history, Downloads, playlists, an album or search, then tap to check more. Play the selection, add it to Favorites, download it, add it to the queue or a playlist, or make a new playlist of it.
- Found lyrics are pinned: listen to a track for half a minute without changing its lyrics, and your other devices show the same lyrics instead of searching again.
- The current line of synced lyrics has a pill behind it that fits the line and glides to the next one.
- The artwork-colored background of Now Playing is ready as soon as it opens, no longer arriving after the lyrics.
