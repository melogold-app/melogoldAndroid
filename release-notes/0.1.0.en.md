The first version of Melogold.

- A new Material 3 Expressive interface: Trends, New, Library, Search and Settings.
- A player with full-screen lyrics, LRCLIB lyrics search and an editor.
- Downloads for listening offline and saving tracks as files to Music.
- An account with a login and a password, and sync of Favorites, playlists and saved albums and artists across devices.
- Updates arrive in the app: "Version … is out" in Settings.
