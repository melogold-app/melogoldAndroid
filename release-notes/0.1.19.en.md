- “Now playing”: a new backdrop — the blurred artwork, alive with the music. It flows, drops of the artwork's colors drift over it (their size follows the share of the color), and on the bass it gathers, zooms in a touch and lights up. Needs Android 13 or newer; older versions keep the previous slow backdrop. It stands still on pause and when system animations are off.
- Settings → Appearance → Player → “Live background”: turn it off and the backdrop stands still.
- Song text: the size of the lines follows the height of the screen, not the width of the column, so lines read the same in any window.
- Fixed: a song stopped midway and skipped to the next one when YouTube stopped serving it after its first megabyte.
- When the network drops or the VPN reconnects, the song waits and continues from the same place instead of skipping to the next one.
- Move the music to another device, like AirPlay: while it plays here, choose a device — the same song continues there from the same second.
