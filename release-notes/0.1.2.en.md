- Lyrics sync through the server: lyrics you made in the editor or imported from a file appear on all your devices, and deleting them on one removes them on the others.
- When no source has synced lyrics for a track but another user of the server made them, their version shows up, marked "Lyrics from the Melogold community".
- The listening history is shared by all your devices. History lets you choose whose plays to show: all devices, this device or another one by name.
- "Remove from history" and "Clear history" apply to every device of the account.
- About shows the version without the RELEASE suffix.
